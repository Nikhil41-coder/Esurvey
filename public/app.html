MAKE ROOM FOR EVERYONE

Build a clearer family story.

  1. Begin with your family head.

    Every person’s relationship is shown relative to this person.

  2. Connect each new person.

    Choose an existing relative, then select child, parent, sibling, or spouse. To add a grandchild, choose their parent and select child.

  3. Explore your connections.

    Select any card to see age, occupation, place, and door number. Search the directory to find someone quickly.

Each person has one primary connection and may have an explicitly selected second parent. Shared children connect to both recorded parents through a common junction. Parenthood is recorded separately from marriage; leave the second parent unspecified when appropriate. Complex relationships are shown as an explicit path from the head.
